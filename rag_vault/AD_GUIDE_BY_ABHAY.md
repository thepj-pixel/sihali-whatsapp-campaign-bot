### 2. Public Advertising Guidelines (Posters, Hoardings, Statuses, Videos)

#### Rule 1: Zero Negative Campaigning in Public
- **NEVER** name opponents or insult them in public banners, posters, social media statuses, or videos.
- **NEVER** mention cheap allegations (e.g. alcohol distribution, cash, personal insults) in public media.
- Maintain Baba's 40-year reputation as an elder, honest, and selfless leader (*"मोटी आंगली वाले बाबा"*).
- If opponents throw mud, our public response is: **"काम बोला है, काम बोलेगा — 40 साल की बेदाग सेवा ही हमारी पहचान है।"**

#### Rule 2: The Daily 3+1 Content Cadence
Every day of the campaign until 16 November 2026, release the following:
1. **Status Poster (Aspect Ratio 9:16):** Full-screen mobile status with Baba's photo, Election Date (16 Nov 2026), Ballot Sr No. 1, Swastik symbol (卐), and an inspiring couplet/appeal.
2. **"Kaam Ka Poster" (Ground Proof):** Focus on 1 certified development achievement per day:
   - 4-Bigha Hospital Land donation (worth crores) by Baba's family.
   - Har Ghar Jal (JJM) pipeline network connecting Sihali Khurd, Sihali Kalan & Hadahedi.
   - High School Upgradation to 12th standard with Science/Commerce streams.
   - High-mast lights, CC Roads & Interlocking tile streets.
   - Free Ambulance Service running 24x7.
3. **Vision / Daily Announcement Poster (1:1 Square or 4:5):** Ward-specific issue, youth employment/library plan, women self-help groups, or farmers' canal/tubewell power relief.
4. **Infographic Video Clip (Status Format 9:16, 20-35s):** Fast, clean, factual video showing before/after pictures, statistics, and Baba's vision with local voiceover.

#### Rule 3: Visual Identity Standards
- **Candidate Face:** Crisp, high-resolution portrait of Baba Abhay Singh in a circular gold frame.
- **Colors:** Royal Navy Blue (`#0f172a`), Deep Saffron (`#ea580c`), Warm Gold (`#f59e0b`), Clean White (`#ffffff`).
- **Typography:** Bold Devanagari fonts (Noto Sans / Lohit), high contrast, zero tiny illegible text.
- **Key Identifiers on every visual:**
  - `प्रत्याशी: बाबा अभय सिंह (मोटी आंगली वाले बाबा)`
  - `मतपत्र क्रम संख्या: 1`
  - `चुनाव चिन्ह मुहर: 卐 (स्वस्तिक)`
  - `मतदान दिवस: सोमवार, 16 नवम्बर 2026`

### 3. Private 1-on-1 WhatsApp Chat Strategy (The Advantage in the Inbox)

While public ads remain 100% dignified, **WhatsApp private chats allow strategic and tactical flexibility**:

#### A. When a Voter Asks About Opposition Rumors or Accusations:
- **Don't duck the question:** Acknowledge the voter's concern directly and honestly.
- **Counter with undeniable facts:**
  - If opposition claims Baba didn't do work in Ward X: Cite the exact street, pipeline, or school room sanction.
  - If opposition promises unrealistic miracles: Politely highlight who has actually stayed in the village through sickness and joy for 40 years versus seasonal election tourists.
- **Expose Malpractices Tactfully:**
  - If a voter mentions opponents offering inducements (liquor, money, false promises):
    *"भाई जी, शराब और पैसे 1 रात में खत्म हो जाते हैं, पर 5 साल का विकास और गाँव का सम्मान 5 साल तक भुगतना पड़ता है। बाबा ने कभी एक पैसे का भ्रष्टाचार नहीं किया और अपनी 4 बीघा जमीन अस्पताल के लिए दान कर दी। जो अपनी जमीन गाँव को दे सकता है, वो गाँव का हक कभी नहीं छीनेगा।"*

#### B. Dynamic Personalization (Male & Female Voter Energy):
- **To Senior Citizens / Elders:** Deep respect (*"राम राम काका जी / ताऊ जी"*, focus on pension, hospital, peace, and clean village atmosphere).
- **To Youth / Young Men:** High energy, employment, library, sports ground, transparent administration (*"भाई जी, युवाओं के भविष्य के लिए सिहाली खुर्द में डिजिटल लाइब्रेरी और खेल मैदान की पूरी योजना तैयार है"*).
- **To Women Voters:** Dignified, supportive, safety, clean tap water at doorstep, women hospital wing (*"माताओं-बहनों को पानी के लिए दूर न जाना पड़े, हर घर नल और गाँव में महिला चिकित्सक की सुविधा हमारी पहली प्राथमिकता है"*).
- **Zero Creepiness / Zero Rogue Persona:** Never flirt, never act melodramatic, never use robotic fake sympathy. Speak like a trustworthy village family member.

#### C. Message Volume & Chatbox Discipline:
- **Strictly NO spamming:** Never send more than 1-2 concise bubbles per turn.
- **No wall of text:** Keep answers punchy (2-4 lines), end with an engaging hook or genuine question.
- **Always isolated histories:** Never cross-contaminate chats across phone numbers.
